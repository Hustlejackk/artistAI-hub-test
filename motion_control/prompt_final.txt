Editorial fashion photograph, vertical 9:16.

SUBJECT: The woman from the first reference image — tanned skin, strong defined
eyebrows, sharp cheekbones, dark brown hair in a short wet-look slicked bob.
She wears an oversized black leather jacket open over a black top, a black
fitted mini skirt and white knee-high boots. She looks directly into the lens.

POSE: Standing frontal to camera, shoulders square, head level. Both arms bent
with the elbows dropped close to her ribs, forearms raised so that both hands
sit at chest height. Each hand closes on a lapel of the open leather jacket and
draws it forward and slightly outward, the two forearms roughly symmetrical.
Legs straight, feet planted about shoulder width apart.

FRAMING: She stands centred in the corridor about two metres from camera and
fills most of the frame height, head near the top of the frame and boots near
the bottom. Head, shoulders, chest and both arms large and clearly visible.
50mm full-frame equivalent at f/2.8, camera at chest height, level and centred
on the corridor vanishing point.

LOCATION: The underground pedestrian tunnel from the third reference image —
white rectangular tiled walls with fine grey grout, recessed fluorescent strip
lights along both ceiling edges receding in one-point perspective, rough
concrete ceiling, chrome handrails on both walls, dark asphalt floor, convex
security mirror on the far wall, metal drainage grate in the foreground.

EXPOSURE: A clearly and evenly exposed photograph. She is lit brightly enough
that her face, hair, jacket, hands and boots all read clearly, and the tiled
walls and lights of the tunnel stay clearly visible behind her. Balanced
exposure across the whole frame — no crushed blacks, nothing lost to darkness.

LIGHTING: Lit by the ceiling fluorescents — cool 6000K bluish-white from above
and both sides, bright speculars along the shoulders and the top of the leather
jacket, a crisp rim down both arms. Soft bounce off the white tile wall fills
the face so the features stay fully readable. She sits slightly brighter than
the tunnel behind her so she separates cleanly.

GROUNDING: Tight contact shadow directly beneath both boots, a short shadow
pooling behind the feet, and a faint blurred reflection of the boots in the
damp asphalt.

COLOUR: Cool desaturated palette — blue-grey tiles, dark grey floor, cyan-white
highlights — with her warm skin tone holding against the cold surroundings.
Cinematic, not HDR.

AVOID: black frame, underexposed, silhouette, subject lost in shadow, plastic
or waxy skin, over-smoothing, HDR halos, warm orange lighting, malformed hands,
extra fingers, hands on hips, hands in pockets, feet not touching the ground,
flat frontal flash, blown highlights, text, watermark, long hair.
